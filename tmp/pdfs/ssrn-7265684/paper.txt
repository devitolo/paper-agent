          Abstract—Around 80 percent of companies are experimenting with generative AI, yet only a fraction of internally developed
          solutions reach production. This paper identifies four systematic anti-patterns – Shadow AI, Pilot Purgatory, Governance
          Vacuum, and Data Quality Deficits – as structural root causes and demonstrates that AI scaling is primarily an architecture and
          governance decision. The key lies in a three-layer architecture comprising a Data & Knowledge Layer, AI Platform Layer, and
          Application Layer, underpinned by federated governance and an Enterprise Architecture Management that evolves from a
          static blueprint into an ongoing steering instrument.                   
          Index Terms—Enterprise AI, Enterprise Architecture Management, AI governance, three-layer architecture, MLOps, Shadow
          AI, pilot purgatory, federated governance, EU AI Act, agentic AI.
